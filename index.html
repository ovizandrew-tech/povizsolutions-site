QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Do my staff or customers need to download an app?

No app store needed. Our platforms open from a link on any phone, tablet or computer, and can be added to the home screen like a normal app.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your business or estate and what you need. We start with a free demo, then give you a clear written quote with no hidden charges.

What happens when there is no light or network?

The till and gate screens keep working offline and sync automatically when the network returns. We also advise on simple backup power.

Is our data and money safe?

Payments go through Paystack or Flutterwave straight into your own account. Staff only see what their role allows, and the systems are designed to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

Can you move our existing records across?

Yes. We import your products, prices, staff and opening stock from spreadsheets or your old system during setup.

Can it work with the gate equipment our estate already has?

Often, yes. We assess your existing barriers, readers and cards during the site visit and reuse what works.
