QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

General
Do my staff or customers need to download an app?

No app store needed. Our platforms open from a link on any smartphone, tablet or computer, and can be added to the home screen like a normal app.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your estate or business and the modules you need. We start with a free demo, then give you a clear written quote with no hidden charges.

What happens when there is no light or network?

Our systems are built for Nigerian conditions. Key screens keep working offline and sync automatically when the network returns, and we advise on simple backup power for critical points.

Who supports us after setup?

Our team in Warri provides on-site training, regular updates and same-day local support by phone, WhatsApp or visit.

Poviz Hub
What kind of businesses is Poviz Hub for?

Restaurants, cafés, juice bars, supermarkets and retail shops, from a single outlet to several branches.

Will sales stop if the internet goes off?

No. The till keeps selling and printing receipts offline, then syncs all sales once the connection is back.

Can I see my sales and stock when I am not at the shop?

Yes. Owners and managers see live sales, stock levels and staff activity for every outlet from their phone.

Can you move our existing records across?

Yes. We help import your products, prices, staff and opening stock from spreadsheets or your old system during setup.

Poviz Estate
Can it work with the gate equipment we already have?

Often, yes. We assess your existing barriers, readers and cards during the site visit and reuse what works.

How do visitors get in?

The resident creates a one-time code and sends it to the visitor by WhatsApp or SMS. The guard checks the code at the gate, and the resident is notified on arrival.

Can residents pay service charges through it?

Yes. Dues are paid in the app through Paystack or Flutterwave, straight into the estate's own account, with receipts and arrears reports for the committee.

Poviz Guard, MinuteDesk and custom projects
How does Poviz Guard prove that guards did their rounds?

Guards check in at set points on each site with their phone, so every patrol is time-stamped. Missed rounds and incidents are flagged to supervisors and clients.

Is MinuteDesk only for large organisations?

No. It suits any office where a director or CEO receives memos and letters that need minuting, tracking and follow-up.

Can you build a website or app for us?

Yes. We build websites, mobile apps and custom systems, including payment integration, hosting and business email setup.
