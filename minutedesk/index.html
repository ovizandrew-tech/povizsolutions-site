The problem

Busy offices lose track of paper

Built from years of experience running a managing director's office, where hundreds of memos and letters pass through every month.

  1. 1
    Memos go missing

    Documents move between desks with no record of where they are.

  2. 2
    Slow decisions

    Directors read long documents with no summary of what is being asked.

  3. 3
    Actions forgotten

    Instructions written on a minute sheet are rarely followed up.

  4. 4
    No history

    Finding what was decided last year means searching through files.

How it helps

From intake to done

Memo intake

Every incoming memo and letter logged with sender, date and subject.

AI summaries

A short summary of each document and what decision is being asked for.

Digital minuting

Directors minute and route documents from their phone or computer.

Action tracking

Deadlines and reminders until each instruction is completed.

Access levels

Confidential items seen only by the people who should see them.

Search and reports

Find any past decision in seconds, with monthly summaries.
