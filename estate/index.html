Poviz Hub Estate

Know who is in your estate, and who is leaving

One platform for gated estates that connects residents, visitors, security guards and the executive committee, so every entry and exit is accounted for.

Register your estate's interestWhat it does
The problem

Paper logbooks can't keep an estate safe

Guards write names they can't check, visitors come and go unrecorded, and the committee only hears about problems after they happen.

  1. 1
    Unverified visitors

    Anyone who gives a resident's name can get in.

  2. 2
    No record of exits

    Nobody can say who left, when, or with what.

  3. 3
    Slow response in emergencies

    No quick way to close every gate or alert residents.

  4. 4
    Dues hard to collect

    Service charges chased by hand, with no clear record.

How it helps

Every gate, resident and visitor, connected

Resident access

Car sticker, card or code at the gate, checked instantly.

Visitor codes

Residents send one-time codes by WhatsApp or SMS. Guards check the code, not just a name.

Secured exits

Every exit is confirmed by the guard on duty and recorded.

Emergency lockdown

Freeze every gate at once, with panic alerts from residents.

Service charges

Dues paid in the app, straight into the estate's account, with receipts.

Staff and reports

Guard rosters, attendance and monthly reports for the committee.

Who it serves

Built for everyone in the estate

  1. 1
    Residents

    Invite visitors in seconds, pay dues and raise alerts from their phone.

  2. 2
    Security guards

    A simple gate screen to check codes and record every entry and exit.

  3. 3
    The executive committee

    Clear reports on access, incidents, staff and dues, every month.

  4. 4
    Facility managers

    One place to manage gates, guards and resident records.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Poviz Hub Estate available now?

It is in development. We are working with a first group of estates to shape it. Register your interest and we will contact you about a pilot.

Can it work with the gate equipment we already have?

Often, yes. We assess your existing barriers, readers and cards during a site visit and reuse what works.

Do residents need a smartphone?

Residents with smartphones use the app. Others can be issued a card or code, and guards can look residents up at the gate.

What happens during a network outage?

Gate screens keep working offline for known residents and codes already issued, and sync when the network returns.
