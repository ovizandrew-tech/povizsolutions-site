The problem

Most food businesses leak money quietly

Not through one big loss, but through small ones every day that nobody can see until month end.

  1. 1
    Cash that doesn't add up

    Sales written in a book or remembered at closing time, with no way to check them.

  2. 2
    Stock that disappears

    Ingredients used, wasted or taken, with no record of which.

  3. 3
    Network and power cuts

    Online systems that stop working right when the shop is busy.

    Owners kept in the dark

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do my staff need to download an app?

No app store needed. Poviz Hub opens from a link on any phone, tablet or computer, and can be added to the home screen like a normal app.

What happens when there is no light or network?

The till keeps working offline and syncs automatically when the network returns. We also advise on simple backup power for the till and printer.

How much does it cost?

A monthly subscription per outlet, based on the features you need. We start with a free demo, then give you a clear written quote with no hidden charges.

Can it handle more than one outlet?

Yes. Each outlet has its own till, stock and staff, and you see all of them together on your phone.

Is our data and money safe?

Payments go straight into your own account. Staff only see what their role allows, and your business data is kept separate from every other business.

Can you move our existing records across?

Yes. We import your products, prices and staff from spreadsheets or your old system during setup.
